Conclusion In summary, ML algorithms were developed to assist the micro- ﬂuidic synthesis of PQDs. The ML models show excellent ability in visualizing of how the synthesis parameter controlled nucle- ation growth ripening mechanisms of PQDs in the conditional prediction maps. They exhibit powerful prediction ability in searching for proper/optimized reaction conditions that synthe- size PQDs with required FWHM–wavelengths. They are ready to be extended to the prediction of more properties such as size dis- tribution and PL quantum yields etc. The ML model could also be adapted to nanomaterials beyond PQDs, facilitating 1) the design and discovery of new nanomaterials; 2) understanding the syn- thesis mechanisms; 3) online quality control; 4) property optimi- zations; and 5) synthesis optimization for time saving, reagent saving, energy saving, and environmental-friendly purposes, thus accelerating the process of research and promoting the industrialization of functional nanomaterials. 4.
